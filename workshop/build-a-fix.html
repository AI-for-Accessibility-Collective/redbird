Build a fix

Describe a change in plain words, and the toolkit builds it. Try these two examples first. Each takes about 10 minutes.

What happens when you press Build it

When you press Build it, the toolkit looks for adapters it already has that make the change. If some fit, you get a skill, a short list of those adapters to turn on together (no new code). If none fit, AI writes a new adapter (the code that makes the change). This takes about 30 seconds. The card under Build it says which one you got.

Example 1 below ends up as a skill, and example 2 as a new adapter.

Example 1: a calmer page for Mark

  1. Open the Pebble charger page (opens in a new tab). Notice the countdown, "Only 2 left in stock" and the scrolling deals bar.
  2. In the panel, open the Build your adapter tab.
  3. In Who is it for?, type: "Mark has ADHD and anxiety. Countdowns and messages like only 2 left make him rush."
  4. In What should change on this page?, type: "Hide the countdowns and only 2 left messages, and stop things from moving."
  5. Press Build it. It turns on adapters from the toolkit, and lists them.
  6. Press Turn off to compare, then turn it back on.
  7. If something is missing, open Not right? Change it, say what to change, and press Change it.
  8. Press Save.

Example 2: star ratings for Sam

Each rating is a picture of stars, so a screen reader only reads the number of ratings, like "(1,203)". No adapter in the toolkit fixes this, so AI writes one.

  1. Open the search results for "charger" (opens in a new tab).
  2. In the Build your adapter tab, replace what's in Who is it for? with: "Sam is blind and shops with VoiceOver."
  3. In What should change on this page?, type: "Read the star ratings out as numbers, like 4.6 out of 5 stars."
  4. Press Point at it on the page, and click the stars under any product.
  5. Press Build it, and wait about half a minute.
  6. Open the list of changes under the result, and press one to see where it is on the page. The stars look the same, since the change is only for the screen reader.

Note

To see the code AI wrote, open Details under the result. You can edit it there, and press Go back to the last version to undo a change.
