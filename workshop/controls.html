Settings you can add to a store link

These are what the controls above set. Add them to the end of any store link after a ?, joined with &. Each one stays set in your browser until you change it, except reset, which happens once.

SettingValuesWhat it does
issueson, agent, off, or numbers like 6,24Which planted problems are on. agent is all of them except the ones that move or change on their own (19, 24, 33, 34, 35) and the two "Add to cart" problems (3, 21).
workshopon, offShows or hides the workshop panel. A link with try in it shows the panel too.
todaya date like 2026-11-02, or nowPins the store's date, so delivery dates are the same on every run. now goes back to the real date.
reset1Empties the cart and the order history.
tryadapter names joined with commas, like swatch-names,calm-urgency, mine, or noneWhich adapters run on the store. mine is the one you write in Write your own adapter.
profilesam, mark, dario, noneTurns on the toolkit's settings for that person.
spotlighton, offPuts a numbered badge on each planted problem on the page.
froman example adapter's name, like swatch-namesCopies that example into "your adapter" in the panel, so you can change it.
