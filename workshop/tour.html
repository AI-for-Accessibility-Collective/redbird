Redbird workshop/Steps/1. Try the toolkit

Try the toolkit

Shop as one of four people and see what the toolkit changes for each of them, then make a profile of your own. This takes about 20 minutes.

Who you can shop as

  • Sam Saltman is blind and shops with VoiceOver.
  • Dario Almondei has a cognitive disability. Long, wordy pages are hard for him to follow.
  • Mark Sugarberg has ADHD and anxiety. Countdowns and "Only 2 left" make him rush.
  • Bill Grapes is 78. Small gray text and small buttons are hard for him, and he worries about paying for things he didn't choose.

Shop as one of them

  1. Open the store as Sam (opens in a new tab), Dario (opens in a new tab), Mark (opens in a new tab) or Bill (opens in a new tab).
  2. In the panel's Profiles tab, read who they are and which adapters are on for them.
  3. At the top of the panel, turn on Show accessibility problems on the page. Each problem gets a red, numbered badge. The ones that shopper's adapters fix turn green, and the ones they fix in only some places turn yellow.
  4. In the Adapter Library tab, turn real-arrival-dates off and on again. The delivery dates go back to "5–7 business days", and badge 31 turns red.
  5. Try to buy something the way they would. Notice what still gets in the way.

Tip

To hear the store the way Sam does, turn on VoiceOver on a Mac with Command-F5, and move with Control-Option and the arrow keys. On Windows, you can use NVDA (opens in a new tab), a free screen reader.

Make your own profile

The four shoppers are made up, so their profiles are only guesses about what people need. The idea of the toolkit is that each person makes their own profile and keeps using it. You can make one for yourself:

  1. In the panel's Profiles tab, pick My profile under Shop as.
  2. Answer the questions in your own words, like what gets in your way on shopping websites and what would make them easier.
  3. Press Make my profile. AI writes your profile from your answers and turns on the adapters that match.
  4. To change it, write what should change under What should change about your profile?, like "Moving banners distract me too", and press Change it. Each change is a new version, and Earlier versions has a way back to each one.
  5. Turn adapters on or off in the Adapter Library tab. The ones you pick are saved to your profile for next time.
  6. Press Save it as a file to keep it. The toolkit's browser extension can load the file with Import profile, so the same profile works on other websites.

Note

Your profile is kept only in your browser. Your answers are sent to AI when you press Make my profile, and your profile with what you wrote when you press Change it.
