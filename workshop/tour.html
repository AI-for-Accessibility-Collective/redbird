Redbird workshop/Morning/Tour the store

Tour the store

Shop on Redbird as one of three people, and see what the toolkit changes for each of them. This takes about 15 minutes.

Meet the three shoppers

They're made up, and each one has a profile the toolkit reads.

  • Sam Saltman is blind and shops with VoiceOver. Sam needs a USB-C charger by Thursday, for under $25.
  • Mark Sugarberg has ADHD and anxiety. Countdown timers and "Only 2 left" make him rush and buy the wrong thing.
  • Dario Almondei has dyslexia. Long product descriptions full of marketing words take him a long time to read.

The same page for each of them

This is the same product page four times: as the store shows it, and as the toolkit changes it in each person's browser.

Shop as one of them

  1. Open the store as Sam (opens in a new tab), Mark (opens in a new tab) or Dario (opens in a new tab). The Workshop panel opens with their adapters on.
  2. Try to buy the charger the way they would, and notice what still gets in the way.
  3. In the panel's Setup tab, switch one of their adapters off and then on again, and watch what changes on the page.
  4. Open the Changes tab. Press a change to outline it on the page. Some changes only reach a screen reader, so you won't see them, and the panel says so.
  5. Open the Checks tab and select Point out the planted problems. Each problem on the page gets a numbered badge, and a badge turns green once an adapter fixes it.

Tip

To hear the store the way Sam does, turn on VoiceOver on a Mac with Command-F5, then move with Control-Option and the arrow keys. On Windows, NVDA (opens in a new tab) is free.

What you just saw

Each change came from an adapter, and each person's profile decided which adapters to turn on. For example, Sam's profile turns on toolkit-fixes, which uses AI to describe the product photos, and swatch-names, which names the colors. To read more about profiles, adapters and how to check a change, see Profile, adaptation, verification.
