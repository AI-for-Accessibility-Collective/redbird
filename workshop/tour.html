Redbird workshop/Steps/1. Try the toolkit

Try the toolkit

Shop as one of four people and see what the toolkit changes for each of them, then shop as yourself with your own toolkit profile. This takes about 20 minutes.

Who you can shop as

  • Sam Saltman is blind and shops with VoiceOver.
  • Dario Almondei has a cognitive disability. Long, wordy pages are hard for him to follow.
  • Mark Sugarberg has ADHD and anxiety. Countdowns and "Only 2 left" make him rush.
  • Bill Grapes is 78. Small gray text and small buttons are hard for him, and he worries about paying for things he didn't choose.

They are made up, so their profiles are only starting points, not descriptions of real people.

Shop as one of them

  1. Open the store as Sam (opens in a new tab).
  2. In the panel's Profiles tab, read who they are and which adaptations are on for them.
  3. At the top of the panel, turn on Show accessibility problems on the page. Each problem gets a red, numbered badge. The ones that shopper's adaptations fix turn green, and the ones they fix in only some places turn yellow.
  4. In the Adapter Library tab, turn real-arrival-dates off and on again. The delivery dates go back to "5–7 business days", and badge 31 turns red.
  5. Try to buy something the way they would. Notice what still gets in the way.

Tip

On a Mac, Command-F5 turns VoiceOver on and off. On Windows, you can use NVDA (opens in a new tab), a free screen reader.

Shop as yourself

The idea of the toolkit is that each person describes what they need once, in their own words, and every app and website they use can follow it. You can try that with your team's account:

  1. Open the extension's sidebar and go to Profile. Under Your profile, describe your abilities in your own words, like "Small, crowded text tires my eyes", or press Speak and say it. Press Save. The toolkit works out what that means for web pages.
  2. Under Suggested for you, the toolkit lists needs that adapters and apps can meet. Redbird's are there too, like Real dates instead of business days and No pressure to buy now. Press Add on the ones you want.
  3. Open the store from the console's Apps page (see Set up the toolkit). In the panel's Profiles tab, Shop as says Your toolkit profile. The store turns on its adaptations that meet your needs, and follows your profile when you change it.
  4. Your built-in adapters run in the extension on every website, not just Redbird. Under Applied on this page in the sidebar, each one has a switch to turn it off on this site, and every adapter has an On switch.

Note

What you write in your profile is sent to the toolkit's AI to understand it, and kept in your team's account. While you shop as your toolkit profile, turning a Redbird adaptation on or off tells your toolkit in one plain sentence, like "Turned on calm-urgency on the Redbird store", so it can suggest changes to your profile. It never sends what is on the page or what you typed.
