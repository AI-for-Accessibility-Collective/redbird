Redbird workshop/Build/Make a skill

Make a skill

Make a skill for Mark without writing code. You describe what he needs, the toolkit picks the adapters, and you try it on the page before you save it. This takes about 20 minutes.

What a skill is

A skill is a short recipe that turns on a few adapters for one need. You write the need in plain words, and the toolkit's Engineer, an AI model, picks adapters to meet it. It can only pick adapters the toolkit already has, so a skill never adds new code.

Make the skill

  1. Open the Pebble charger page (opens in a new tab) with no adapters on. Notice the deals bar that keeps scrolling, the countdown, and "Only 2 left in stock".
  2. In the Workshop panel, open the Build tab.
  3. In Who are you building for?, type: "Mark has ADHD and anxiety. Countdown timers and messages like only 2 left make him rush and buy the wrong thing."
  4. Make sure Make a skill is selected. In What does this person need on this page?, type: "Calm the page down. No countdowns or pressure messages, and nothing moving."
  5. Press Pick adapters for me. After a few seconds, you'll see the adapters the Engineer picked, with what each one does.
  6. Press Try it on this page. The adapters turn on, and you can check whether the page is calmer.
  7. If something is missing, write it under Not quite right? Say what to change and press Ask for a change. For example, "Also make the text bigger". To drop an adapter you don't want, press Take out next to it.
  8. Give the skill a short name and press Save skill. It shows up under Your skills, where you can turn it on again later.

Read the SKILL.md

Under your skill, press SKILL.md. This is the same format the toolkit uses for its own skills:

  • The top part, between the two --- lines, has the skill's name and one sentence on what it does and when to use it. An AI agent reads this sentence to decide whether the skill fits a page.
  • The middle sections say what each adapter does, in plain words.
  • The Recipe at the bottom is the list of adapters the toolkit actually turns on.

Other ways to make a skill

  • Switch adapters on yourself in the Setup tab. Then, in the Build tab, press Use the ones on now.
  • If the Engineer says no adapter does what the person needs, press Describe it as a fix, and AI writes a new adapter instead. That's the next guide, Describe a fix.
