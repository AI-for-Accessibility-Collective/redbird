Pick an issue to fix

No adapter in the toolkit fixes these issues yet. Pick one, start from the template, and open the store with that issue turned on.

Profiles

Adapters get the profile as options.profile. To load one, add &profile= and its name to the address. These three are made up:

  • dario: Dario Almondei has dyslexia and prefers short, plain text at about a 6th grade level (dario.json (opens in a new tab))
  • mark: Mark Sugarberg has ADHD and anxiety, and wants pressure messages hidden (mark.json (opens in a new tab))
  • sam: Sam Saltman is blind and shops with VoiceOver (sam.json (opens in a new tab))
