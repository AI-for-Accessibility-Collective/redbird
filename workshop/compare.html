One store, three people

This shows the same product page four times: as Redbird shows it, and as each of the three profiles gets it once the toolkit adapts it. The store itself stays the same, and the adapters change the page for each person reading it.

Note

The views below are small copies of the real page, with the video's sound turned off. To use one, open it in its own tab. The photo descriptions and the rewritten text come from the AI, so give them a few seconds.

Shop as one of them
