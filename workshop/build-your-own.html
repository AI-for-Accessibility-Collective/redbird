Build your own

Build a fix for the need your group chose in the morning, with the same two builders. Plan for about 40 minutes.

Start from your card game

Have these three things from the morning sessions:

  • Who it's for: the ability profile. Write it in the Build tab's Who are you building for? box.
  • What to change: the adaptations you settled on, and the pages they're on.
  • How you'll check it: your verification plan. You'll use it in Test it and share it.

Skill or adapter?

  1. Try Make a skill first. Describe the need and see which adapters the Engineer picks.
  2. If the adapters do the job, try the skill, change it until it's right, and save it.
  3. If the Engineer says no adapter does this, or its adapters don't quite fit, press Describe it as a fix and have AI write a new adapter.
  4. A fix can also be both, e.g., a skill that turns on your adapter together with two of the toolkit's.

Tip

Describe one change at a time, and say where on the page it is. For example, "Let me add more time to the checkout timer" works better than "Make checkout easier". Use Point at it on the page whenever you can.
