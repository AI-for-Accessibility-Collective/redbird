Redbird workshop/Steps/4. Build your own

Build your own

Build a fix for the person and the problem your group picked in the morning. Plan for about 40 minutes.

What to build

Pick one problem that the person your group is designing for runs into on Redbird, and describe one change that would help. For example, "read the star ratings out as numbers" rather than "make the page accessible".

  • If the toolkit's built-in adapters already make the change, the assistant suggests them. Your job is to check that they are the right ones, with the right settings.
  • If not, it writes a new adapter. Your job is to test it and ask for changes until it works.

Who does what

If someone on your team has the access need you're designing for, they should test each version with their own tools (e.g., their screen reader) and say what's still wrong. If not, one person on the team uses the store with the same tools the person would use (e.g., VoiceOver or only the keyboard). Someone else talks to the assistant in the extension's Builder, or to a coding agent (see Build it in code).

Start from your card game

  1. Open the store on the page where the problem is.
  2. In the extension's Builder, press New chat. Say who the fix is for and what gets in their way, in plain words, like the person would say it. Then say the one change you want.
  3. Answer the assistant's questions, and share the page when it asks.
  4. Check the preview the way the person would (e.g., with VoiceOver or with only the keyboard). If it's not right, say what's wrong under Additional requests?. Keep going until the person can finish the task on the page with their own tools.
  5. When it works, press Add to my account. Build the next change in a new chat.

Tip

Describe one change at a time, and say where on the page it is. For example, "Give me more time at checkout" works better than "Make checkout easier".
