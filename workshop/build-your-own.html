Build your own

Build a fix for the person and the problem your group picked in the morning. Plan for about 40 minutes.

What to build

Pick one problem that the person your group is designing for runs into on Redbird, and describe one change that would help. For example, "read the star ratings out as numbers" rather than "make the page accessible".

  • If the toolkit already has adapters that make the change, you get a skill, which is a list of those adapters to turn on for that person. Your job is to check that they are the right ones.
  • If not, AI writes a new adapter, the code that makes the change. Your job is to test it and ask for changes until it works.

Most groups end up with one saved fix that combines a toolkit adapter or two with one new adapter.

Who does what

If someone on your team has the access need you're designing for, they test each version with their own tools, like their screen reader, and say what's still wrong. If not, one person plays them as closely as you can. Someone else types the changes into the panel, or asks the coding agent for them.

Start from your card game

  1. Open the store on the page where the problem is.
  2. In the Build your adapter tab, describe the person you're building for under Who is it for?. If you're stuck, open Not sure what to write?, answer the three questions and press Write it for me. If you're building for yourself, make your own profile first. Its text is filled in under Who is it for?.
  3. Write one change under What should change on this page?. If you're not sure what to change, open Not sure what to change? and press Suggest changes to get three ideas for this page.
  4. Press Build it.
  5. Check the page the way the person would, for example with VoiceOver or with only the keyboard. If it's not right, write what's wrong under What should change next? and press Change it. Keep going until it works for them.
  6. When it works, press Save. Build the next change the same way.

To keep going in code, open Keep building in code under your fix and download it as a folder for Claude Code or Codex. Keep building in code has the steps.

Tip

Describe one change at a time, and say where on the page it is. For example, "Give me more time at checkout" works better than "Make checkout easier". Use Point at it on the page whenever you can.
