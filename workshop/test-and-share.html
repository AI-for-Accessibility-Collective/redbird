Test it and share it

Check your fix against your verification plan, then share it for the demos. This takes about 30 minutes.

Test it

Go through the checks in your verification plan. These are the ways to check on Redbird:

What to checkHow
It changed what you wantedIn the Builder, open the preview's line in the chat to see its checks and what it changed.
It didn't break anythingTurn Apply previews off and on again, and look at the whole page. Once it's in your account, use its switch under Applied on this page in the sidebar's Profile tab.
The review found nothingThe Builder shows how the background review is going, and the assistant fixes what it finds. In code, the agent's verify report says the same.
A screen reader gets itIn the store panel's Audit tab, open What a screen reader hears, or turn on VoiceOver with Command-F5.
It works with only the keyboardPut the mouse away and use Tab, Shift-Tab, Enter and the arrow keys.
The problems went awayTurn on Show accessibility problems on the page at the top of the store's panel, and check that your problem's badge turns green.
It works on other pagesOpen the search results, another product and checkout, and check again.

If a check fails, tell the assistant what's wrong under Additional requests?, or tell your coding agent.

Share it

  1. In the developer console, open Adapters and press Share on your fix. (In code, the agent can do this for you.)
  2. Say its name in the workshop chat.
  3. Other teams find it in their console under Shared with you, and press Run in my browser. It stays on the version they installed until they choose Update.

Note

A shared fix is code that runs in the browser of whoever installs it. The toolkit refuses code that sends data over the network, but that is not a sandbox. Read a fix's code before you run someone else's.

For the demo to the group

  1. Say who you built the fix for, and what on the page got in their way.
  2. Turn your fix off with its switch in the sidebar, and show the page as the store has it. Then turn it back on and show what changed.
  3. Show one check from your verification plan. For example, turn on VoiceOver and let the group hear what a screen reader says now.
