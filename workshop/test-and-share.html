Test it and share it

Check your fix against your verification plan, then share a link to it for the demos. This takes about 30 minutes.

Test against your plan

Go through each check in your verification plan. These are the ways to check on Redbird:

What to checkHow
It changed what you wantedIn the Changes tab, read each change before and after. Press one to outline it on the page.
It didn't change anything elseRead the rest of the Changes list. Then switch your fix off and on in the Setup tab, and compare the page.
A screen reader gets itRead What a screen reader hears at the bottom of the Changes tab, or turn on VoiceOver with Command-F5.
It works with only the keyboardPut the mouse away and use Tab, Shift-Tab, Enter and the arrow keys.
The problems went awayIn the Checks tab, press Run the toolkit's checks with your fix off, then again with it on. Each count shows what it was before. You can also select Point out the planted problems and see which badges turn green.
It works on other pagesOpen the search results, another product and checkout, and check again.

If a check fails, go back to the Build tab and say what's wrong, under Not quite right? for a skill or Change something for an adapter.

Share it

  1. In the Build tab, press Copy link under your skill in Your skills, or under your adapter.
  2. Paste the link in the workshop chat.
  3. When someone opens it, the panel shows your skill or adapter in their Build tab. A skill's adapters turn on right away. An adapter only runs after they read it and press Run it as your adapter.

Note

The link carries the whole skill or adapter, so nothing is uploaded and nobody needs an account. That's also why the link is long.

Get ready for the demo

  • Say who the fix is for and what got in their way.
  • Show the page with your fix off, then on. The switch is in the Setup tab.
  • Show one check from your verification plan, e.g., what a screen reader hears now.

After the workshop

What you made uses the toolkit's own formats. A SKILL.md has the same shape as the toolkit's built-in skills in toolkit/skills/builtin (opens in a new tab), and an adapter has the same enable() and disable() as the ones in tools/adapters (opens in a new tab). To keep your adapter as a file, see Write an adapter by hand.
