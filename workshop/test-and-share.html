Test it and share it

Check your fix against your verification plan, then share it for the demos. This takes about 30 minutes.

Test it

Go through the checks in your verification plan. These are the ways to check on Redbird:

What to checkHow
It changed what you wantedUnder your fix, open the list of changes and press each one to see it on the page.
It didn't break anythingPress Turn off to compare, then turn it back on, and look at the whole page.
A screen reader gets itIn Audit, open What a screen reader hears, or turn on VoiceOver with Command-F5.
It works with only the keyboardPut the mouse away and use Tab, Shift-Tab, Enter and the arrow keys.
The problems went awayTurn on Show accessibility problems on the page at the top of the panel, and check that your problem's badge turns green.
It works on other pagesOpen the search results, another product and checkout, and check again.

If a check fails, write what's wrong under What should change next? and press Change it. If you're working in a folder, send it to the coding agent from Your folder instead, and ask the agent to turn the check into a test (see Keep building in code).

Share it

  1. Go to the page your fix changes. In the Build your adapter tab, under Saved fixes, press Share next to your fix. The link is copied. If you're working in a folder, press Share it under Your folder instead.
  2. Paste it in the workshop chat.
  3. Your fix shows up in the Build your adapter tab of whoever opens the link. If the fix has an adapter AI wrote, that adapter only runs after the person opening the link reads its code and presses Run it.

For the demo to the group

  1. Say who you built the fix for, and what on the page got in their way.
  2. Press Turn off to compare under your fix, and show the page as the store has it. Then turn it back on and show what changed.
  3. Show one check from your verification plan. For example, turn on VoiceOver and let the group hear what a screen reader says now.
